Google Search — auction metrics

Weekly, Mon–Sun, from the Google Ads account (campaign-level impression share). Brand Search = the Search campaigns with “Brand” in the name (Brand and Brand_Broad); All search = every Rocket Mobile Search campaign. Each segment is the campaigns’ shares weighted by eligible impressions (impressions ÷ impression share), so they behave like a single combined campaign. Google reports anything below 10% as “< 10%”, which is shown here as ~10%, so low shares are slightly overstated. Search IS + share lost to rank + share lost to budget = 100%. Top and absolute-top IS are the share of eligible impressions shown above the organic results / in the very first position. Competitor-level auction insights (overlap, outranking share) aren’t available from the Google Ads API, so they’re not included.

Methodology
  • Purchases (GA4) = the GA4 “Purchase” key event (ecommercePurchases, equal to conversions filtered to eventName = purchase) by channel, property 282813955, from October 2026 onwards; counted as-is, no accuracy ratio.
